Meeting Purpose

Align YHQ and YBX on the long-term strategic direction, the future YBX-affiliate operating model, product portfolio readiness, delivery capability transition, commercial governance, financial performance, risks, management reporting capabilities, and key decisions required to move forward.

The Rome sessions will focus on strategic alignment and decision-making. The Acireale sessions will combine the Press Conference for the presentation of the 2026–2028 Industrial Plan with internal working sessions and operational deep dives.
